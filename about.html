Careers↗
01

Safety:

We consider safety at work a supreme value that cannot be compromised, we work towards a safety culture that protects the safety of everyone and ensures that we all return home safely.

Safety is at the center of every action and process in the company. We are committed to creating a safe and healthy work environment for every employee, and to providing all the tools and training required to do so. We expect every employee to show personal responsibility, to act with the utmost caution and to report any potential hazard or danger.

02

Quality and professionalism:

We strive for excellence in everything we do, while uncompromisingly adhering to the highest level of quality. We believe that professionalism is expressed not only in results, but also in the way we operate – with mutual respect, responsibility and a constant pursuit of learning. Every employee in the company is an ambassador of these values, and is committed to them in front of the work interfaces, customers and partners. Together, we create an environment of trust, excellence and achievements, which drives the growth and success of us all.

03

Innovation and Sustainability:

We are committed to continuous innovation, striving to develop and implement groundbreaking solutions in the field of renewable energy, to lead the market. We consider sustainability a core value that guides every decision we make. We act with a deep responsibility towards the future, and are committed to leaving a positive mark on the environment and future generations. Together, we build a greener future through advanced technology in the light of sustainability.
